failure/submission events required by those five features. They must not be
reported as “all 17 fired” for this smoke.

Private engine state is not inert by definition; it is counted separately from
model-visible payloads. Only a recorded downstream actuation, compiler decision,
or provider delivery is evidence of later-decision use.
